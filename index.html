Who we are

A small firm that buys at a discount, adds value with its own hands on the project, and exits on its own terms.

Queenshill was founded by Chris Price in 2014 after a career in financial services. The model has not changed since: find residential sites that others have overlooked or cannot unlock, buy them at a meaningful discount to completed value, take them through planning and construction with a hand-picked professional team, and sell, let or refinance at the right moment.

Our investors are listed corporates, family offices and ultra-high-net-worth individuals. They are paid first. Queenshill takes its profit only after investors have received their prioritised return, which keeps every decision on site aligned with the people whose capital is at work.

Eight schemes were delivered in London and Surrey between 2014 and 2021, from a converted MOT garage in Brixton to a 47-apartment building in Tottenham Hale. Since 2022 the same discipline has been applied to prime and super-prime residential on Portugal’s Lisbon coast and in the Algarve, working through Portuguese companies with local delivery partners.

How we work

Risk is managed before the purchase, not after it.

Most development losses are made on the day the site is bought. So we are hard to please at acquisition: every site is underwritten on a conservative appraisal against a strict internal discount to completed value, and if it does not clear that bar we do not chase it, however good it looks.

Everything after that is about keeping the plan on the rails. Industry-leading architects, cost consultants and employer’s agents are appointed on every scheme, construction is procured on fixed terms with retentions and late penalties, and investors receive a prioritised return ahead of Queenshill’s own profit.

Source

Off-market and probate stock, land assembly and tired buildings, found through a network built over twelve years rather than through agents’ mailing lists.

Underwrite

Base and conservative cases on every deal. Independent valuation and agent evidence before exchange, never a hoped-for end value.

Plan

Planning and licensing handled with specialist consultants. Several schemes were sold with planning secured, taking construction risk off the table entirely.

Build

Fixed-price contracts, employer’s agent oversight, weekly site attention and written approval for every variation.

Exit

Sell into a prepared market, or let and refinance to hold the asset and release partner capital. The route is decided at underwriting, not at the end.
